# Fieldproof Revenue Atlas

Create a brand new editorial blog website for Atlas Intelligence based on the atlas-ai-os.com software to create product interest and awareness, with an image for each blog article post, using the current Atlas branding throughout.

Use the attached Project Atlas logo and approved editorial photography for the branding, featured imagery, and article visuals.

Follow this implementation specification:

ATLAS INTELLIGENCE
MASTER IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT: EVIDENCE-TO-SUPPLEMENT EDITORIAL PILOT

MISSION
Transform Atlas Intelligence into a focused editorial and lead-generation experience for insurance restoration and roofing businesses.
The priority is a carefully executed four-article pilot centered on one commercially important operational problem:
Legitimate restoration work can become harder to justify, bill, or recover when field documentation does not adequately support the associated estimate line items.
The objective is to help restoration businesses improve the connection between field evidence, estimating, supplement preparation, and project closeout while establishing Atlas as a credible operational intelligence partner.

The pilot must combine:
- Four authoritative, actionable articles with an editorial image for each post.
- A downloadable resource for each article.
- A dedicated video player experience for Article 1.
- A single primary conversion action: "Apply for the Atlas Revenue Recovery Pilot".
- A working pilot application journey.
- Privacy-conscious analytics.
- A 30-day measurement framework and reporting dashboard.

1. BRANDING & VISUAL IDENTITY
Use Project Atlas branding throughout: dark technical palette, blue/cyan/purple accents matching the Project Atlas logo, refined typography, and the attached editorial photography.

2. FOUR-ARTICLE PILOT CLUSTER
Publish the following four articles as the core editorial cluster:

ARTICLE 1: FLAGSHIP GUIDE
Title: "The 5 Photographic Gaps That Slashing Adjusters Look For (And How to Close Them)"
Audience: Restoration owners, project managers, estimators, and supplement specialists.
Content: 5 clearly numbered documentation gaps (missing overall context, missing close-ups, missing scale/measurements, missing before-and-after/concealed conditions, missing links between photos and estimate line items). Include weak vs. strong photo comparisons, practical remediation steps, common mistakes, and the primary Atlas Revenue Recovery Pilot CTA.
Downloadable resource: "Atlas Intelligence: The 5-Point Field Photo Checklist" (field-friendly printable/downloadable format).
Media: Embedded 16:9 landscape companion video player explaining the 5 gaps.

ARTICLE 2: TACTICAL LINE-ITEM WALKTHROUGH
Title: "Connecting Photos to Scope: How to Build Defensible Supplement Justifications"
Audience: Estimators and supplement coordinators.
Content: Photo quantity vs. evidence quality, connecting photos to estimate line items, documenting measurements, referencing verified building codes and manufacturer specs, organizing an evidence package for professional review, side-by-side illustrative comparison (Poorly organized evidence package vs. structured evidence mapping).
Downloadable resource: "Atlas Intelligence: Photo-to-Line-Item Evidence Mapping Template".

ARTICLE 3: FINANCIAL AND CLOSEOUT REVIEW
Title: "The 45-Minute Job Closeout Audit: Finding Revenue Hiding in Completed Work"
Audience: Restoration executives, business owners, operations leaders, and revenue recovery managers.
Content: Reconciling completed work against billing, the realistic 45-minute timed review protocol (stages summing to 45 mins), separating documented discrepancies from assumptions, avoiding unsupported statistics.
Downloadable resource: "Atlas Intelligence: 45-Minute Job Closeout Audit Checklist".

ARTICLE 4: OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW
Title: "Field-to-Desk Handoffs: Standardizing Evidence Without Burdening Field Techs"
Audience: Restoration operations managers, production managers, field supervisors.
Content: Standardizing evidence requirements without administrative overload, photo naming/tagging conventions, capture rules, escalation protocols, handoff criteria.
Downloadable resource: "Atlas Intelligence: Field-to-Desk Evidence Handoff SOP".

3. SINGLE PRIMARY CTA & PILOT APPLICATION FLOW
- Primary CTA: "Apply for the Atlas Revenue Recovery Pilot" ("Run 5 of your past or in-progress claim files through our evidence review framework to identify potential missed scope and unbilled line items").
- Working application form collecting: Full Name, Business Email, Company Name, Website, Role, Company Type, Monthly/Annual Claim Volume, Primary Documentation/Revenue Recovery Challenge, and Consent.
- Instant submission feedback, form validation, and an internal/admin review view for applications.

4. DOWNLOADABLE RESOURCES
Provide downloadable resources for each of the 4 articles with print/export functionality and clean layout.

5. ANALYTICS & MEASUREMENT DASHBOARD
Include tracking for article views, engaged reading time, scroll depth, downloads, CTA clicks, application submissions, and video interactions, with an internal 30-day pilot performance reporting dashboard evaluating Gate 1 (Resonance), Gate 2 (Commercial Intent), and Gate 3 (Video ROI).
